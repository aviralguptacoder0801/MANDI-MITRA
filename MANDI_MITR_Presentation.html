The Problem

India's farmers are losing billions — and nobody is fixing it right

700 million farmers. Broken market access. Zero price transparency.

🧅

Middlemen Exploitation

Farmers sell at ₹2/kg while consumers pay ₹30/kg. 90% margin eaten by middlemen with zero value addition.

No Price Visibility

Farmers have zero real-time mandi price data. They accept any price the buyer offers, often under distress.

Mandi Congestion

Our Solution

Mandi Mitr — The Complete Farmer's Digital Companion

Mandi Mitr is a full-stack React + TypeScript + Bun web platform that acts as a smart, bilingual bridge between farmers, mandis, and buyers — giving every farmer the same market intelligence that was previously only available to large agri-corporations.

🔊
Voice & WhatsApp access — farmers with no smartphone literacy can still get price alerts and mandi data via IVR voice calls and WhatsApp messages in Hindi/regional languages.
🤖
AI Chatbot (Gemini-powered) — answers farmer queries in their local language, provides crop price forecasts and selling recommendations.
📈
Real-time price dashboard — live mandi price feed, trend charts, best-mandi recommender.
🚦
Mandi congestion intelligence — know queue wait times before you drive 50 km to the mandi.
⭐
Profit grade calculator — instantly evaluates if today's price is worth selling at.
🌐
Community forum — peer knowledge sharing, collective bargaining, and expert advice.

Feature Deep Dive

How Each Feature Solves a Real Problem

🤖 AI Chatbot (Gemini)

FloatingAiChatbot.tsx — Powered by Google Gemini API
  • Understands natural Hindi/regional language queries
  • Answers: "आज गेहूं का भाव क्या है?" in real time
  • Gives personalized sell/hold recommendations
  • Provides crop advisory and weather-based alerts
  • Persistent floating widget — always accessible

📞 Voice & WhatsApp Bot

VoiceWhatsAppWorkspace.tsx — Bridging the Digital Divide
  • Works on feature phones via missed-call + IVR
  • WhatsApp price alerts in farmer's language
  • No smartphone or internet required for basic access
  • Covers the 60%+ farmers without smartphones
  • Scheduled daily price broadcasts to subscribed numbers

🚦 Mandi Congestion Pooling

CongestionPoolWorkspace.tsx — Save Time & Fuel
  • Crowd-sourced real-time queue updates from farmers
  • Predicts best time to visit each APMC mandi
  • Carpooling: match farmers going to same mandi
  • Reduces fuel cost and waiting time by 40–60%
  • Gamified reporting with community points

⭐ Profit Grade Engine

ProfitGradeWorkspace.tsx — Smart Sell Decisions
  • Input: crop, quantity, location, expected price
  • Compares against 7-day moving average price
  • Grades deal: 🟢 Excellent / 🟡 Fair / 🔴 Wait
  • Factors in transport cost and storage viability
  • Prevents distress selling at below-MSP rates

💬 Community Forum

CommunityForumWorkspace.tsx — Farmer Social Network
  • Hyperlocal posts: region, crop, and language filtered
  • Price discovery through peer crowd-verification
  • Expert agri-specialists answer questions
  • Group buying / collective selling coordination
  • Disease & pest alerts shared in real time

🌐 Region & Language Bar

RegionLanguageBar.tsx — Truly Inclusive Design
  • One-click language switch: Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi, Telugu
  • Automatic region detection → shows local mandi prices
  • Fonts and UI adapt for right-to-left and Devanagari
  • No English required anywhere in the app
  • Context-aware regional commodity defaults
